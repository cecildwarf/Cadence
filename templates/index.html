    Queue — one build at a time

    Books build in order, one at a time, so a small server never runs two encodes at once. Every row shows live progress: a bar, a % and the current stage. Tap Edit to fix a queued book's title or order before it starts.

    Building / queued

      Needs order review

      Multi-file books land here first — open one, check the auto-sort, then confirm to join the queue.

        🔍 Review — health-held books

        After every build, Cadence checks the finished file against the book's published length and chapter list. A file that fails or looks wrong never goes to Plex — it waits here, in the Review folder, until you decide. Approve places it in Plex anyway (and records what you overrode) — only then do the originals move to Processed. Reject deletes the held file — the originals stay in their original source folder, so you can re-process the book from there.

          Cleanup — free disk space, safely

          Processed originals are only listed as safe to delete when the finished .m4b exists in Plex, plays fine, matches the originals' length within 2%, and its job finished Done. Anything unsure lands in "Needs review" with the reason why — never on the delete list. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.
